## Why `launch-seo-safety.md` in particular should not be read as current

page, on page-level averages suggesting it was the strongest of the three. The
per-page query exports pulled later that day showed all three pages averaging
position 37–38 with no clear winner, and that the consolidation onto
`/our-services/procurement/` was already live on production. `seo-action-list.md`
§1.2 carries the corrected reading.
